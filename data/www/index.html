Feeds

Feed edits, including the order, are not stored until you save. Drag the grip or use the arrows to reorder; the feed on screen finishes its turn before the new order starts. In the ticker the first half of the list scrolls along the top and the second half below, so the order also picks the section.

Export writes the feed list to a file you can import again after a reflash - pio run -t uploadfs rewrites the whole filesystem partition, which erases the saved config.

Cities

Each city gets two screens on the panel: the weather now, then the next five days. The name is what the panel shows - edit it to shorten a long one. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, which needs no key, and refresh every 15 minutes.

Weather screens

Whether the weather shows at all is Show on the Display tab.

Clock

The time zone is one of your weather cities', so daylight saving is followed automatically. Seconds on screen is how long the clock stays up between the other parts; on its own it stays up for good. Turn the clock on with Show on the Display tab.

Display

The panel goes through whatever is ticked, in this order. With the tickers on, the weather and the clock play once after every full pass through them. With the tickers off, prices are not fetched at all; one thing on its own just stays up.

Santa flies across every two to five minutes while it snows, the first time shortly after the theme comes on.

0 scrolls continuously. Above 0 the list holds each time an entry reaches the top, then rises one - easier to read, since vertical motion moves every character at once.

Mode, theme and the sliders apply to the panel straight away - save to keep them across a restart. Snow falls behind the figures in every mode; its color is SNOW in the palette.

The percentage is always the 24 hour change, whatever span the chart covers - so it stays comparable between feeds. The chart is coloured by its own direction, so the two can disagree.

Panel: -. Refresh runs on DMA with no CPU involved, so nothing the device does can make it flicker. Colour depth is fixed by the firmware build.

WiFi

Off keeps the radio awake rather than waking it for every beacon, for steadier fetching. It costs about 40 mA, which is nothing on mains power.

Erases the stored network and restarts into setup mode.

Market data

Single + chart fetches only the feed on screen, as its turn comes up; both tickers fetch every feed in one request per source - Binance, CoinGecko and up to 20 Yahoo tickers each take one - so the poll interval costs the same at thirty feeds as at three.

Stops every price request until resumed or the device restarts. Not saved.

Only needed for coingecko feeds. Its free tier allows roughly 10k calls a month, so keep the poll interval high - coingecko is for coins Binance does not list, not for fast updates. Stored on the device, never sent back to this page.

Palette

Colours apply to the panel immediately; save to keep them. Reset to factory brings back the defaults. Switch to the palette screen to see them all side by side, or leave it on a ticker or the chart to judge them in place.

Ramp values left to right: 255, 180, 130, 95, 70, 50, 35, 25.

Maintenance

Resets tickers, display settings and colours to factory - BTC, ETH, SOL, ZEC. WiFi credentials are untouched.
